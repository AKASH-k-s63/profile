Principles

Engineering Philosophy

01

Design for Failure

Every system will fail. Great engineering anticipates failure modes, builds graceful degradation, and ensures recovery is faster than the incident itself.

02

Automate Ruthlessly

If a human is doing it more than twice, it's a process waiting to be automated. Automation is not laziness — it's engineering discipline.

03

Measure Everything

Opinions are cheap. Metrics are truth. If you can't measure it, you can't improve it. Observability is a first-class engineering requirement.

04

Ship, Then Iterate

Perfect is the enemy of deployed. Deliver working systems, observe real behavior, and iterate based on evidence — not assumptions.

05

APIs Are Contracts

A REST API is a promise to your consumers. Breaking that contract breaks trust. Version thoughtfully, deprecate gracefully, document obsessively.

06

Understand the Why

Before touching a system, understand its purpose. Code without context creates technical debt. Engineering with intent creates systems that last.
